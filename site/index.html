Pro reports turn scans into client-ready deliverables

The free CLI is enough for quick checks. Pro reports are designed for founders, agencies, and consultants who need to explain risk to a team and hand over a remediation plan.

What the Pro report adds

  • Executive summary for non-security stakeholders.
  • Prioritized high-risk findings table.
  • Remediation checklist for MCP configs.
  • Server-by-server risk detail.
  • Local-only export; configs are not uploaded.
